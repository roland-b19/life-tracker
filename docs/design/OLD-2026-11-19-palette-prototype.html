How color is used

The accent (turquoise) appears only on

progress bars and rings, the 6px dot of the active tab, nav item or segment, the primary button, the focus ring, and on switches and checkboxes when they are on. Everything else is --text or --secondary; links are --text, underlined.

Status colors are never text

A status is a subtle fill, a dot and a word in --text. Labels on a colored fill use --on-tint.

What stays the same

No new features, screens or layout changes; no animation beyond the existing ones; the font; the corner radius; the muscle map's group colors; icons and the tab bar position. The mode is not synced across devices, and the browser bar color follows the system even when the switch picks the other mode.

Build steps (for the new session)

  1. Token block with light-dark(), the page glow and the contrast test.
  2. Every color literal becomes a token; accent and status colors leave text; data-URI icons become masks.
  3. Axe scans in both color schemes (four Playwright projects).
  4. Surfaces: hairline and shadow once on the shared card; the tab bar takes the segmented shape; round checkboxes.
  5. "Alive" levers: big numerals, the accent rule, badges as fill + dot + word, a clearer weight hierarchy.
  6. The Auto / Light / Dark switch in an always-shown Settings, stored per device, applied before the first paint, with Undo.
  7. Viewport, manifest, error page and app icons.
  8. The owner checks the iOS status bar and splash on a preview.
  9. Apply that result.
  10. Muscle map 3D grey and lights in light mode, if needed.
  11. Docs and rules ("dark only" goes), status, log and index.
