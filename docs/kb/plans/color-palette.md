# Plan: color palette

## Goal
The owner opens the app all day (food, gym, tasks) and finds the current Graphite look flat and monochrome, like "no rush". The app should feel serious, motivating, energetic and orderly, in the style of Apple's native apps, with one accent on every page. This plan changes colors, surfaces and type weight only: no new features, no animation.

A clickable prototype of the result (one basic view of each page, dark and light, built with the tokens below) is the owner's private artifact "Life Tracker Palette" (https://claude.ai/artifact/5QjGoVdnwXR1tZexqesGoU). Use it as a picture of the colors, surfaces and type weight only: its page layouts, sample data and widths are simplified, and the app's structure stays as it is (the owner: change the aesthetic, not the structure). Where it and this page differ, this page wins. After the build the owner tests the app and sends small fixes to the building session.

## Scope
- In: new color tokens in `app/globals.css`, each written once with `light-dark()`, for a dark and a light mode that follow the system (D-020); a manual Auto / Light / Dark switch (D-024); glass cards with a hairline edge and a soft shadow (D-021); the bottom tab bar in the shape of the segmented selector (`.segmented`, like Health's Today / Shopping), not a pill; round, uniform checkboxes; the "alive" levers: big numerals for key metrics, the accent only for progress, the active state and the primary action, a clearer weight hierarchy, status colors used as fills with a word, never as text (D-022); the viewport, manifest, error page and app icon colors; both color schemes in the axe scans; the rules and docs that say "dark only".
- Out: animation (a separate later iteration; the existing animation exceptions stay); the font (D-023); the muscle map's group colors; syncing the mode choice across devices; different accents per page; new screens, new features or layout changes; the rounded corner radius; any new dependency.

- D-021: translucent glass cards with a hairline and a soft shadow
- D-022: "alive" without animation: big numerals, accent only where it means something, clearer weights, functional status colors (classic green), colors never used as text
- D-023: the font stays SF Mono
- D-024: the owner's answers to the eleven open choices (teal-leaning turquoise, subtle edge, numerals `clamp(2rem, 9vw, 2.7rem)`, classic green, manual switch too, dark splash and recolored icon, the owner checks the status bar, plan approval is the OK for the rule rewrite, the switch in an always-shown Settings, kept per device, golden light amber)

- Where styles go (owner's rule, applies to every step): set a style once on the general structure (`:root`, `body`, the shared card, control and page-container classes) or on the direct container, and let children inherit it through tokens and `inherit`; no per-page or per-element copies of the same declaration. The smallest amount of CSS wins. Before adding a rule, check whether an existing shared class or token already covers it.
- Color rule (step 6): accent and status colors are fills, dots, bars, borders and icons, never text. `--tint` stays only on progress bars and fills, the small dot on the active tab, nav item or segment, the primary button and the focus ring. Every other `--tint*` use (about 43) moves to `--text` or `--secondary`; links are `--text`, underlined. A label on a colored fill is `--on-tint` (`#04201b`, dark in both modes); `--on-tint` never sits on `--danger` (4.2:1 in light). Status badges are a `-subtle` fill, a small dot of the status color and a `--text` word. Destructive buttons are `--danger-subtle` with a `--text` label. As built in step 6: the key numerals share one rule (`.metric-big`, `.item-total` and `.macro-numbers`; every macro since D-029), and `.macro-target` beside them keeps normal letter spacing; the active nav item (sidebar and More menu) is the segmented thumb (`--surface-solid` with `--card-shadow`) with the 6px `--tint` dot at its end, in place of the `--tint-subtle` fill; the dot goes on the status badges only (`warning` and `danger`; `info` and `rest` are not statuses); the destructive button's hover mixes `--text` into `--danger-subtle`; titles (`.app-title`, `.section-title`) go to weight 800, beside the 800 key values and the 600 labels.
- Success color: a classic green (answer 4), apart from the turquoise by hue at the same brightness.
- Checkboxes: one round style, a 20px circle with a 1.5px `--secondary` border; done is a `--tint` fill with a `--on-tint` tick drawn the same way everywhere.
- Icons drawn as data-URI SVGs (the select chevron, `app/globals.css` line 813, stroke `%23a1a9b2`; the checkbox tick, line 843, stroke `%230b1b30`) cannot use `var()`: the tick becomes a `mask` of the same SVG on the checkbox's `::before`, whose `background-color` is `--on-tint`. A `<select>` holds no pseudo-element and a wrapper would change the markup, so the chevron (built in step 3) is two `linear-gradient` strokes in `--secondary`, the same 10 × 5px shape; `--chevron-end` on `select` sets its inset from the right.
- Segmented control and tab bar (as in the design artifact): one shared rule for `.segmented` and `.tabbar`. The track is `--surface-secondary` on `.segmented` and the glass surface on `.tabbar`; the active item is a raised neutral thumb (`--surface-solid` with `--card-shadow`) with a `--text` label and a 6px `--tint` dot drawn with `::after` (`.segmented button::before` is already the 44pt hit area, `app/globals.css` line 1301), beside the label in `.segmented` and above it in `.tabbar`; inactive labels are `--secondary`. The `--tint` thumb with `--on-tint` label on `.segmented` (`app/globals.css` lines 1348 and 1389) and the tab bar's pill radius and `--tint-subtle` thumb go. The tab bar keeps its icons and position. As built in step 5: the tab bar's dot is a `radial-gradient` on the thumb (`.tabbar::before`), not an `::after` on the item, because while the More menu is open both the page's tab and More carry `.active` and two dots would show; the dot rides with the thumb and hides with it (`data-idle`). The tab item is 3.5rem tall with more room above the icon for the dot, and the More menu and the phone toast sit 0.25rem higher to keep their gap. The `.segmented` dot is a flex item of the button (`button` is `inline-flex`), so it has `flex: none` and the button's gap sets its distance from the label. `display: grid` stays on `.segmented` and `.tabbar` themselves, not the shared rule, so the desktop rule that hides the tab bar still wins. Changed after the build (D-025, D-026): the tab bar, the More menu and the sidebar have no dot; the tab bar swaps the segmented control's colors (the bar `--surface-solid` with no blur, its flat thumb `--surface-secondary`), the More menu is `--surface-solid` like the bar, and the selected `.nav-link` is `--surface-secondary`; the tab bar has the More menu's 1.25rem corners and a 0.9375rem thumb, and the More menu's links are one centered column; the in-page `.segmented` keeps its dot and its corners.
- The switch's `localStorage` key is `lt-theme` (the `lt-` prefix of `lt-folded` in `app/components/categories-card.tsx`), holding `light` or `dark`; Auto removes the key. The Undo toast uses `undoWith` from `app/components/toast.tsx`, the way the categories card does.
- Browser bar color with a manual mode: it follows the system through the media-query `themeColor` (step 8), even when the switch picks the other mode. A script cannot hold the meta tags: every `router.refresh()` removes and re-adds the tags React hoists into `<head>` (D-019), so a script's change would snap back after the first write. A small known limit; the page itself always follows the switch.
- If a light-mode axe scan fails because of text over a translucent card, the builder first raises the light `--surface` alpha (up to a solid card) and only then changes a token value, by the smallest step that passes, updating the table below.

## Contrast pairs
`tests/palette-contrast.test.ts` reads every `light-dark(<light>, <dark>)` in each token of `:root` in `app/globals.css` (a token may hold several, one per color stop) and checks every pair in both modes. A translucent color is composited over the solid card, the worst case for text on a glass card; for text on the page, the worst case is the page color with each glow color composited at its full alpha (the glow's peak). Values today (dark / light) are in brackets.
- Text, at least 4.5:1:
  - `--text` on `--surface-solid`, `--surface-modal`, `--surface-secondary` and `--background` (10.4 / 14.4 or more)
  - `--secondary` on the same four (4.7 / 5.9 or more)
  - `--text` and `--secondary` on `--background` with the glow color (`--glow`, D-029) composited at its peak (4.6 / 4.8 or more)
  - `--text` and `--secondary` on `--tint-subtle`, `--success-subtle`, `--warning-subtle` and `--danger-subtle` over `--surface-solid` (4.7 / 4.7 or more)
  - `--on-tint` on `--tint`, `--tint-hover`, `--success` and `--warning` (6.7 / 4.9 or more)
- Colored fills, at least 3:1: `--tint`, `--success`, `--warning` and `--danger` on `--surface-solid` and `--background` (5.0 / 3.1 or more). The one exception is light `--warning` (2.9 on the card, 2.6 on the page) (choice 11): the test holds it to at least 2.5, with a comment naming choice 11.
- Not paired: colored fills on `--surface-secondary` (fields and tracks; 2.9 in light), so a progress bar's track is a `-subtle` fill or `--separator`, not `--surface-secondary`.
