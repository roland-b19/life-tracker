# App idea (raw, from the user)

Status: **brainstorm input, not a spec.** The detailed plan comes out of a question-and-answer round with the user and ends up in `docs/spec.md`. Sections may be changed or removed. No implementation until the plan is agreed.

## Concept

A "life tracker": a notes app on steroids. Several separate modules for different life areas, each with functionality a normal notes app does not have. Simple but complete, intuitive, fast to use. Multi-user, with notifications and alerts. Apple-level look and feel (see the `apple-design` skill). Must also satisfy the Azure course requirements (`docs/requirements.md`). After the course it is used personally.

## Modules (user's first list, English summary)

1. **Health**: general health info; data from Apple Health / Apple Fitness (how to integrate is to be investigated).
2. **Money**: log every purchase fast (as few taps as possible): category, subscriptions, food, clothes, etc.; tracking per category.
3. **Daily to-do**.
4. **Future projects / development**: personal or professional growth.
5. **Gym**: workout split, per-workout journal, progress charts; also other sports (running, padel, hiking, other activities).
6. **Work opportunities**: companies, people, ideas, notes.
7. **Wedding**: log wedding items, import an Excel file and show a dashboard from it.
8. **Chess**: learn chess.
9. **AI integration**: find where and how AI fits, ideally using one of the user's existing subscriptions.
10. **Business**: dashboard for the family business: to-do, spendings, earnings, everything useful for a business.
11. **Food**: meal prep, planning, daily macros, daily body-weight weigh-in, shopping list.
12. **Wishlist**: gifts, things to buy or to receive.

## Cross-cutting requirements

- Multi-user, notifications, alerts.
- Design from Apple's public design resources (Human Interface Guidelines), as a complete skill usable by any agent.
- Ask the user questions per module and for the rest of the app, and propose ways to elevate the app for real users.
